The concept

JanyuTech is an Indian OEM building custom robots that take people out of hazardous work in the core industries that build the nation.

We specialize in pioneering custom-designed robotics and cutting-edge solutions to reduce human risk in the core manufacturing sector. Our products are expertly crafted to enhance compliance with ESG commitments and the principles of Industry 4.0, ensuring a safer, more efficient, and environmentally conscious manufacturing environment.
