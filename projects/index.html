EPC Activities

JanyuTech offers comprehensive EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) services tailored to meet the complex needs of various industrial sectors, including oil & gas, chemicals, food processing, and utilities. With a team of highly qualified engineers, we ensure optimal plant configuration, meticulous procurement of essential materials, and efficient erection and commissioning processes. Our services extend beyond project completion, providing integrated operation and maintenance solutions to guarantee long-term reliability and efficiency. Committed to quality and cost-effectiveness, JanyuTech is a trusted partner for delivering end-to-end industrial solutions globally.

Project Summary Report: EPC Activities of Egyptian Indian Polyester Company (EIPET)

Overview

The Egyptian Indian Polyester Company S.A.E. (EIPET) is a joint venture primarily involving Dhunseri Petrochem & Tea Limited from India (70% share), Egyptian Petrochemicals Holding Company (23% share), and Engineering for Petroleum & Process Industries (7% share). The project focuses on producing bottle-grade PET resin at its site in the Industrial Development Area North-West of Suez, Ain Sokhna, Suez Governorate, Egypt. The plant occupies a land area of 141,000 square meters and operates as a private free zone.

Conclusion

EIPET’s project in Ain Sokhna represents a significant investment in the production of PET resin, leveraging international partnerships and advanced engineering solutions. The successful execution of the EPC activities has been pivotal in establishing a robust production facility capable of meeting high demand across multiple global markets.
