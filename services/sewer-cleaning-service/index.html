Sewer Cleaning Robot as a service

Sewer Cleaning Robots

Regular sewer cleaning is a must because it helps maintain public health and protect the environment while ensuring proper wastewater treatment. It prevents structural damage and helps identify the issues in the sewage pipes before the situation gets out of hand.

However, such humans get exposed to toxic particles in the sewage which leads to respiratory problems, infections, and other health hazards. Here’s where JanyuTech comes to the rescue by offering a robotic sewer cleaning machine that ensures the cleanliness, functionality, and safety of urban sewer systems.

RAAS- Sewer cleaning Robot (SCE/SCH)

  • Suction Hose: Use negative pressure to remove materials.
  • Lateral Washing System: No need for a cleanout for the laterals. Includes handgun arrangement.
  • Accessories: High pressure water jets, cameras and sensors, and more.
  • Crawler: To reach difficult areas for more cleanliness.
  • Adjusting: Can move in both directions to adjust into smaller places.
  • Versatility: Can adapt to different sewer systems and environments.
  • Remote Operation: Humans do not go into or near the sewer to operate the robot.
  • Durability: Can withstand challenging conditions for longer periods.

Support Offered By JanyuTech

The comprehensive support offered by JanyuTech with sewage cleaning robots are:

  • Maintenance Services: Regular inspections and repairs for the sewer cleaning robot.
  • Customization: Adding equipment to suit various sewer systems.
  • Technology Integration: Use of AI for autonomous operations, cameras for inspection, and more.
  • Robot Design: Sewage cleaning robot are tailored to go through sewage pipes, carrying cleaning equipment along with it.

BENEFITS OF RAAS

The following are the benefits of Robots As A Service (RAAS):

  • Cost Effective: This robotic automation is to be used as a service, not purchased.
  • No Maintenance Costs: The maintenance costs of the robots are borne by us and not our customers.
  • No Infrastructure Overhead: RAAS works on cloud infrastructure, reducing the overhead.
  • Easy Upgrade: Renting an upgraded robotic solution is cheaper than buying a new robot.
