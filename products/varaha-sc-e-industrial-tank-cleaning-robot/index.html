Varaha SC-E Industrial Tank Cleaning Robot

Varaha SC-E - Industrial Tank Cleaning Robot

Varaha SC-E is a Remotely Operated system for removing the dirt, algae, rust flakes and slurry of Water Storage Tanks. The ETP, Sump and storage tanks require regular maintenance to ensure the sanitary levels of the water being stored and hence has a direct impact on the health of the citizens.

  • SCE is a lightweight ROV with Single phase suction pump mounted on ROV.
  • SCE can reach upto 30 Meter inside the tank with supplied 30 Meter waterproof power cable and communication cable.
  • SCE comes with the SCE graphical user interface (GUI) application for tractable operation.
  • For lowering the machine inside the tank 50m steel wire tripod and mounting hooks are provided on the ROV.
  • In front side brush assembly is given for the cleaning with the reducer for the suction pipe.
