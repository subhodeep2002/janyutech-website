Steel Industry

Scroll

Though one of the key industries in the country, the steel industry faces challenges related to rust and corrosion removal. Also, the emissions or waste produced in this industry have to be carefully handled. However, robot manufacturers in Mumbai, India, like JanyuTech, address these concerns and provide ideal solutions by providing robots for various applications.

Robotic Removal of Hot Scales from Reheating Furnace

The Thee 500 is a high-temperature, remotely operated robotic system developed to automate the removal of scale deposits from the confined under-canals of Hot Steel Mills (HSMs) and reheating furnaces. Designed for environments with intense radiant heat and limited human accessibility, the crawler features a tethered hydraulic drive, heat-shielded body and an articulated robotic arm that breaks the deposits with precision.

  • Seamless navigation beneath furnace structures due to the compact form
  • Integrated camera and lighting system for safe and accurate remote operation
  • Fully compatible for efficient outward transport of collected material
  • Reduces downtimes significantly up to 2-3 days
  • Can be deployed at around 500° C

Online Remotely Operated Pipeline Cleaning Robotic System

It is a strong, high-power robotic unit designed for internal descaling and debris removal in pipelines.   • Operable from a remote station to work in confined and high-risk areas

Sludge Cleaning

We offer three specialised robotic solutions for efficient sludge management in steel plants. The SCE (Sludge Cleaning ROV – Electric) is ideal for light to moderate sludge in confined tanks, offering precision and low-power operation. The SCH (Sludge Cleaning ROV – Hydraulic) dislodges and pumps out dense, abrasive sludges in deep or high-temperature pits. For the most demanding environments, RHINO is a heavy-duty, track-based system designed to extract and transport large volumes of industrial sludge across uneven terrain.

  • Together, these systems minimise downtime
  • They reduce manual exposure and do not remove water
  • The volume of sludge pumped out is monitored by a flowmeter
  • The data is made available online
