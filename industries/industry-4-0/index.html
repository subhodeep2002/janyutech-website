Automation and Industry 4.0 Solutions

From production to processing and packaging to warehousing, robots have entered the industries as a part of automation. Moreover, the robot manufacturers in India can provide robots that in turn provide services such as pick-and-place, carrying goods throughout the warehouse, helping in the production and assembly line and more. Thus, we provide RAAS (Robotics as a Service) to work for you so you can focus on your business growth.

Automated Cement Bag Wagon Loading System

The Automated Cement Bag Wagon Loader is a mechanised system designed to streamline the loading of cement bags into railway wagons, eliminating the need for manual stacking. It features a master conveyor that extends into the wagon and a baby conveyor that shifts bags laterally for precise placement, as one of the industry 4.0 automation solutions.

  • Arrangement of bags in groups of eight
  • Defined stacking pattern
  • Conveyor lifting to form multiple rows
  • Ability to load a wagon in just 30-40 minutes
  • Safer, faster and efficient loading process

Pick-n-Place Robotics Arms

The Pick-and-Place Robotic Arms are precision-controlled, multi-axis systems designed for high-speed material handling, assembly and packaging operations across industrial settings. Built with servo-actuated joints, customisable end-effectors and a robust control interface, these arms ensure accurate object recognition, gripping and placement with minimal cycle time. The system integrates with existing automation lines and supports vision-based feedback for dynamic object tracking.

  • Designed for harsh shop-floor environments, improving throughput
  • Can handle repetitive, hazardous or challenging tasks for humans
  • Available in various payload and reach configurations
  • More consistency, speed and operational safety
  • Ideal for electronics, logistics, automotive, manufacturing, etc sectors

Factory Automation

The Gantry Pick-and-Place Robot is a precision automation solution designed for high-speed, repetitive material handling in industrial environments. The system features a bridge-style gantry structure that enables linear movement across X, Y and Z axes, offering full coverage of the working area. Mounted with a servo-driven manipulator and gripper, it ensures reliable gripping and placement of components with minimal cycle time.

  • Integrated with limit sensors, safety interlocks and a manual override console
  • Supports both automated and semi-automated operations
  • Ideal for assembly lines, quality control stations and packaging units
  • More throughput, reduced human fatigue and consistent precision

Custom Made Automatic Guided Vehicles (AGVs)

Our Custom-Made AGVs are the robotic transport systems for material handling, intralogistics and shop-floor automation across diverse industrial environments. These autonomous, programmable units navigate using magnetic tape, LiDAR or vision-based guidance, enabling flexible path planning and obstacle avoidance. Designed with modular chassis options, the AGVs can be configured for payloads ranging from lightweight components to multi-ton assemblies.

  • Integrated with IoT dashboards, safety bumpers and precision docking systems
  • Suitable for warehouses, production lines and cleanroom facilities
  • Ideal for lean manufacturing, pharma, automotive and electronics sectors
  • They optimise manpower and reduce handling time
