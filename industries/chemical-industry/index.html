Chemical Industry

Scroll

The chemical industry turns out to be a boon for turning raw materials into chemical products. But the stubborn and harmful chemical residues, safety standards and inaccessible areas cannot be overlooked. These problems can be solved by using robots for cleaning applications, especially when provided by a service robot company in Mumbai, India, like JanyuTech.

Explore Our Products

Reactor Vessel Cleaning

The Reactor Vessel Cleaning System is a compact, remotely operated solution designed to clean high-risk, confined chemical reactor interiors without manual entry. It integrates a directional high-pressure jetting mechanism mounted on an adjustable, articulating arm. It delivers 360° rotational cleaning inside vertical and horizontal vessels, dislodging hardened chemical residues and sludge from curved, high-surface-area interiors.

  • Deployed through existing manways
  • Compatible with inert atmosphere conditions
  • Ideal for batch reactors and large storage vessels
  • Minimal human intervention and safe and efficient cleaning
  • Reduced downtime, safety risks and chemical exposure

Settlling Tank Cleaning

Our SCE (Electric ROV) is ideal for routine cleaning in smaller tanks with lighter sludge and controlled environments. The SCH (Hydraulic ROV) tackles dense, high-viscosity sludge in larger or reactive tanks, built to withstand corrosive conditions. For high-volume, high-risk cleaning operations, RHINO provides heavy-duty performance across large sludge basins and outdoor pits.

  • All systems are remotely operated
  • Minimised human exposure and reduced downtime
  • Regulatory compliance in critical chemical sector operations
  • Ideal for complex, hazardous sludge in effluent and holding tanks
  • Precision, safety and chemical resistance
Get a Quote→

Pipeline Inspection

The Pipeline Cleaning Robot is a compact, remotely operated system designed to navigate complex and obstructed pipe interiors. Built for water, sewer and wastewater applications, it integrates a high-precision drive system and front-facing rotary cleaning head to dislodge debris and sediment. The robot operates via a Graphical User Interface (GUI) and includes Software Capability Evaluation (SCE) for controlled, real-time movements.

  • Lightweight and modular design
  • Can be deployed via narrow access points and varying pipe diameters
  • Ideal for inspection, cleaning and maintenance
  • Suitable for pipelines, ducts and HVAC systems
  • Ensures uninterrupted flow in critical infrastructure networks
