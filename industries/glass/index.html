Glass Industry

The glass industry, through manufacturing, flourishes other industries such as construction and housing. However, energy-efficient manufacturing, reduction of wastage and gentle handling are required here. These challenges can be solved by using robotics as a service provided by a service robot company in Mumbai or India, and JanyuTech stands as a leading company in this regard.

01 / 02

Lehr Roller Cleaning

Our Lehr Roller Cleaning System is a precision-engineered, remotely operated solution for cleaning roller beds in annealing lehr furnaces within glass manufacturing units. Over time, lehr rollers accumulate fine glass dust, oil and thermal residue – leading to quality defects and operational inefficiencies. This system employs a motorised trolley-mounted cleaning module capable of traversing parallel roller beds with minimal human intervention.

  • High-efficiency brushing or blasting mechanisms for uniform cleaning
  • No shutdown needed for the entire line
  • Ideal for high-temperature tolerance and narrow clearances
  • Reduced maintenance downtime and enhanced product quality
  • Promotes operational safety in float glass and sheet glass production facilities

AI Enabled Vision Based Robotic Automation System for Glass Yarn Manufacturing

The Automated Bobbin Handling and Palletising System is a fully automated solution designed to unload bobbins from trolleys, stretch wrap them and transport them to the palletising area with minimal human intervention. The operator is only required for the bobbin joining process. With dual independent lines, the system ensures modularity and continuous operation, handling up to 3600 bobbins per 7-hour shift efficiently.

  • Vision-based robotic unloader to accommodate trolley variations
  • Automatic stretch wrapping machine
  • Flexible conveyor with a 64-bobbin buffer
  • Robotic palletiser for precise stacking
