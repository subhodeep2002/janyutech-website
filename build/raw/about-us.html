About us

Welcome to JanyuTech

Janyutech is an Indian OEM specializing in the design and manufacturing of Human-Enabling Collaborative Robots (cobots) tailored for hazardous, high-load, and repetitive industrial applications. Our systems are engineered for maximum operational safety, efficiency, and productivity, combining advanced robotics with user-centric design. Built with a modular and compact architecture, our cobots deliver high dexterity and can operate effectively in spatially constrained and complex environments. They are designed for rapid deployment, featuring intuitive user interfaces that allow unskilled operators to manage them with minimal training. With an inherently safe design, our robots eliminate the need for physical safety barriers, enabling direct human-robot interaction.
We also provide comprehensive robotics solutions including robotic inspection and maintenance (specializing in confined spaces and sludge removal), high-temperature and harsh-environment operations, and materials handling automation. Our turnkey services cover end-to-end system integration, from design and customization to deployment and maintenance. Through our Robots-as-a-Service (RaaS) model, clients gain flexible, scalable access to advanced robotics without large capital investments. At Janyutech, we are committed to delivering intelligent, accessible, and safe automation technologies that enhance human capability and transform industrial operations.

Advantages of our Robotic Solutions and Industrial Automation Systems:

  • Ease in data retrieval and recovery ensuring data integrity
  • Precision process design with seamless handling
  • Minimized human interface with good amount of manpower saving
  • High scale production output with increase in capacity utilization
  • Minimized variation on output with reduction in rework/rejection
  • Assurance on safety and quality
  • Regulatory and quality compliance
  • Inclusion of value added features
  • Enhancement of packaging standard periodic training of the people involved in the process
  • Fine tuning the parameters and upgrading the controls

Our Vision

Janyutech aspires to be a leading force in human risk-mitigating robotics, with a strong focus on the core manufacturing sector. Our mission is to transform India’s industrial landscape through our 3P model:

People – enhancing human safety by reducing exposure to hazardous and strenuous tasks.

Profits – boosting plant profitability by increasing operational efficiency, minimizing downtime, and ensuring consistent quality.

Planet – promoting sustainable practices by optimizing resource consumption and reducing environmental impact. 

Our Goals

  • To understand and deliver robotic and automation solutions tailored to the unique needs of our clients, their customers, and their respective industries.
  • To reduce the incidence of major industrial accidents and enhance worker safety in strenuous industrial environments.
  • To boost client profitability while upholding ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) commitments by increasing resource use efficiency.
  • To build and nurture a positive and collaborative working environment with our stakeholders, customers, and business partners to ensure collective wellbeing.
  • To foster strong, effective partnerships with vendors, suppliers, and agents to support the successful execution of customer projects.
